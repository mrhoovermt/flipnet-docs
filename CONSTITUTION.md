# FLIPNET Founding Constitution

*Freedom. Liberty. Independence. Prosperity.*

*This belongs to everyone.*

---

## PREAMBLE

We believe the internet is the greatest communication tool in human history — the modern equivalent of the printing press. Like the press before it, it holds the power to catalyze reformation, scientific revolution, and enlightenment. Instead, it has been captured by corporate greed, weaponized by algorithmic manipulation, and turned into an engine of division, addiction, and extraction.

FLIPNET exists to reclaim that promise.

We believe that people, when able to speak freely and honestly to one another, find more common ground than division. We believe that verified identity, community self-governance, and radical transparency can replace anonymous manipulation and algorithmic exploitation. We believe the infrastructure of human communication should serve humanity — not shareholders.

FLIPNET is not a product to be sold. It is a permanent public resource, constitutionally protected from capture, built to outlast any individual, any administration, and any era of politics. Its mission cannot be sold, redirected, or diluted. It belongs to its community — forever.

This Constitution is the founding covenant between FLIPNET and every person who joins it. It defines what FLIPNET is, what it can never become, and the rights and responsibilities of every member of this community.

## ARTICLE I: NAME, MISSION, AND PERMANENCE

**Section 1.1 — Name**

This organization shall be known as FLIPNET, an acronym for Freedom, Liberty, Independence, and Prosperity Network.

**Section 1.2 — Mission Statement**

FLIPNET's mission is to provide a permanently free, community-governed, manipulation-free platform for human communication and collaboration — one that distributes the surplus value it creates back to the communities that create it.

**Section 1.3 — Governing Entity**

FLIPNET is owned and operated by FLIPNET LLC (Oregon Registry #255242399), a single-member limited liability company. The Founding Chair commits to the eventual transfer of all FLIPNET assets, intellectual property, and governance authority to a Swiss Foundation (Stiftung), structured as a purpose-locked perpetual entity under Swiss law (Schweizerisches Zivilgesetzbuch, ZGB Art. 80–89). Until that transfer is complete, FLIPNET LLC operates the platform in trust for the eventual Foundation, bound by the principles of this Constitution.

The transition to the Foundation shall occur when:

- A credible Board of Directors has been identified and is prepared to assume governance
- The platform has demonstrated operational stability sufficient to sustain Foundation oversight
- The Founding Chair has secured the legal and financial resources required for Swiss Foundation formation

There is no fixed deadline for the transition under normal circumstances. Once formed, the Foundation's purpose, as defined in this Constitution, shall be protected from alteration, redirection, or dissolution to the fullest extent permitted under Swiss law. No individual, corporation, government, or investor shall hold ownership over FLIPNET or its platform.

**Compelled transition trigger.** Notwithstanding the foregoing, if FLIPNET LLC retains sole ownership of the platform for more than ten (10) years from the date of the one-hundredth (100th) verified-member signup, an external trustee designated in the LLC operating agreement shall have the authority to compel Stiftung formation, drawing on the Stiftung formation fund as it then exists. The Founding Chair retains the right to appoint the initial Board upon compelled formation. The trustee's authority to invoke this trigger is unilateral and may be exercised without the Founding Chair's consent, including in the event of the Founding Chair's incapacity, unwillingness, or death.

This provision exists as a structural defense against the most common failure mode in founder-to-foundation transitions: the indefinite postponement of "ready." The clock begins at the one-hundredth verified member because that is the moment at which the platform has demonstrably acquired a community to whom the non-extractive promise is owed; ten years from that moment is the outer bound of any defensible "we are still preparing the transition" claim. The Founding Chair drafted and accepted this provision in advance precisely to ensure the mission outlives the Founding Chair's authority over it.

**Section 1.4 — Operational Entities**

FLIPNET LLC, the current operational entity, is governed by this Constitution as the binding covenant between FLIPNET and its users, even prior to Foundation formation. Upon Foundation formation, FLIPNET LLC shall continue as a subsidiary operational entity under the Foundation's authority, or be reorganized as the Foundation determines appropriate.

The Foundation, once formed, may establish or authorize additional operational entities to conduct day-to-day business in other jurisdictions. All operational entities shall:

- Operate under the authority of and in subordination to the Foundation
- Be bound by the mission, prohibitions, and governance requirements of this Constitution
- Hold platform intellectual property in trust for the Foundation, not as independent assets
- Maintain separate financial records subject to Foundation audit

The Foundation retains the right to reorganize, replace, or dissolve any operational entity, provided such action does not compromise platform continuity or user rights.

**Section 1.5 — Irrevocable Prohibitions**

The following are permanently prohibited and cannot be authorized by any board, vote, amendment, or court action short of full dissolution:

- Sale of FLIPNET, its platform, its data, or its infrastructure to any private entity
- Transfer of governing authority to any single individual or corporation
- Use of behavioral user data for advertising targeting of any kind
- Algorithmic content manipulation designed to maximize engagement over user wellbeing
- Suppression of verified user speech for political, commercial, or governmental benefit, except as required by applicable law pursuant to Article XIV
- Issuance of equity, shares, or ownership stakes of any kind
- Sale, licensing, or transfer of individual user identity data to any third party for any purpose
- Premium features that create a different platform experience for paying users versus non-paying users

## ARTICLE II: GOVERNING STRUCTURE

**Section 2.1 — The Board of Directors**

Once the Foundation is formed, FLIPNET shall be governed by a Board of Directors consisting of no fewer than five (5) and no more than eleven (11) members. The Board is the highest governing authority of FLIPNET, subject to the constraints of this Constitution.

Prior to Foundation formation, the Founding Chair holds governing authority as the sole member of FLIPNET LLC, bound by the commitments in this Constitution.

**Section 2.2 — Board Quorum and Decision-Making**

A quorum for Board business shall consist of a simple majority of seated Board members. Ordinary decisions require a simple majority vote of the quorum. Decisions involving budget approval, staff appointments, Constitutional interpretation, or platform architecture changes require a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority of seated Board members.

**Section 2.3 — Founding Board and Founding Chair**

The Founding Chair shall appoint the initial Board of Directors at the time of Foundation formation, subject to confirmation by a four-fifths (4/5) supermajority of seated Board members once the Board reaches five (5) members. The Founding Chair shall serve as Chair of the Board for an initial term of five (5) years from the Foundation's formation (the "Founding Period"). This Founding Period exists to establish platform culture, governance infrastructure, and community standards before transitioning to full community election.

**Section 2.4 — Founding Chair Override**

During the Founding Period, the Founding Chair holds executive authority over platform operations and governance decisions. However, a four-fifths (4/5) supermajority of the full Board may override any decision of the Founding Chair, including appointments, operational directives, and policy decisions. This override power exists as a check against unilateral authority, not as a routine governance mechanism. The Founding Chair retains veto power over Board decisions that would conflict with this Constitution, subject to the same 4/5 override.

**LLC-era external trustee dissent power.** Prior to Foundation formation, when no Board exists, the override mechanism above is not yet operative. To prevent the LLC period from being an interval of unconstrained unilateral authority, an external trustee designated in the LLC operating agreement shall hold a public dissent power, exercisable as follows:

- The trustee may issue a public statement of Constitutional concern with any Founding Chair decision they consider Constitutionally suspect or contrary to the mission as expressed in Article I.
- Issuance of such a statement automatically triggers a community-wide review vote on the decision, open to all verified members.
- The trustee's dissent is non-binding; the community vote is advisory; but both are public and recorded in the `governance_decisions` table.
- The community vote does not override the Founding Chair's decision. Its purpose is to surface the disagreement, create a public record, and give the community a documented voice during the LLC period before formal Board governance exists.
- The trustee may exercise this power at most four (4) times per calendar year, to prevent its abuse as a substitute for normal disagreement.
- The trustee is the same external trustee designated in §1.3 for the compelled transition trigger. Their identity is specified in the LLC operating agreement.

This power is intentionally weak in mechanical terms — the Founding Chair retains final authority during the LLC period — but strong in transparency terms. It guarantees that no decision made during the LLC period can be made in silence, and that a named external party with no operational stake has standing to publicly question it.

**Section 2.5 — Elected Board Transition**

Beginning no later than five (5) years after the Foundation's formation, no fewer than two-thirds (2/3) of Board seats shall be filled by community election. The remaining seats shall be appointed by the Board itself through a nomination and confirmation process requiring a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority, with the purpose of ensuring continuity, institutional knowledge, and specialized expertise (legal, financial, technical). Elections shall be held annually. Board members shall serve staggered three (3) year terms to ensure continuity and prevent full board capture in any single election cycle.

**Section 2.6 — Board Member Eligibility**

Any verified FLIPNET member in good standing, with a positive Ethos score in at least one community and a minimum of one (1) year of platform membership, may stand for election to the Board. Specific eligibility thresholds shall be defined in the public Ethos Methodology Document maintained by the Board. No Board member may simultaneously hold a position with any company that directly competes with FLIPNET's core mission. Board members must disclose all material conflicts of interest upon candidacy and on an ongoing basis.

**Section 2.7 — Term Limits**

No individual may serve more than three (3) consecutive elected terms on the Board. After a mandatory one-term absence, a former Board member may stand for election again. Appointed Board members are subject to the same term limits. Service during the Founding Period does not count toward elected term limits.

**Section 2.8 — Board Compensation**

Board members may receive reasonable compensation for their service, as determined by an independent compensation committee and approved by community vote during the annual election cycle. Compensation shall be publicly disclosed. No Board member may receive compensation that creates a financial dependency on FLIPNET sufficient to compromise independent judgment.

**Section 2.9 — Conflict of Interest**

Any Board member with a material personal, financial, or professional interest in a matter before the Board shall disclose the conflict and recuse themselves from deliberation and voting on that matter. Failure to disclose a material conflict constitutes grounds for removal. The Board shall maintain a publicly available conflict of interest register.

**Section 2.10 — Community Recall**

Any elected Board member may be recalled by the community. A recall petition must gather signatures from no less than fifteen percent (15%) of verified active users. Upon validation, a recall vote shall be held within thirty (30) days. Removal requires a supermajority of sixty-seven percent (67%) of votes cast. A special election to fill the vacancy shall be held within sixty (60) days. The Founding Chair is exempt from community recall during the Founding Period but may be removed by a four-fifths (4/5) supermajority of the full Board for cause.

**Section 2.11 — Board Responsibilities**

The Board of Directors shall be responsible for:

- Stewardship of the FLIPNET mission and this Constitution
- Approval of annual operating budgets and financial reserves
- Oversight of the Community Distribution Fund
- Appointment and oversight of platform leadership and staff
- Adjudication of major appeals escalated from community moderation
- Authorization of any changes to platform infrastructure or governance systems
- Compliance with all applicable laws and regulations across operating jurisdictions

## ARTICLE III: USER IDENTITY AND VERIFICATION

**Section 3.1 — Verified Identity Principle**

FLIPNET is built on the principle that authentic human communication requires authentic human identity. Every FLIPNET account that participates on the platform must correspond to a real, living, government-verified individual. No bots. No anonymous accounts. No duplicate identities. No multi-account abuse.

**Section 3.2 — Verification Model**

FLIPNET implements a single verification standard for platform participation: government-issued identity verification through a privacy-preserving third-party verification service. Verification establishes that an account belongs to a real human being and that the human meets the platform's age requirements.

The verification fee, currently set at $5 USD per year (or local equivalent), covers the underlying cost of identity verification, payment processing, and a small operational margin. The fee shall be set at a level that maintains accessibility while functioning as a meaningful spam moat. Adjustments to the fee require Board approval and community disclosure.

To accommodate users who genuinely cannot afford the verification fee, FLIPNET shall maintain a community sponsorship program: a verified user in good standing may sponsor verification for one (1) other user per calendar year. The one-per-year cap is structural and prevents sponsorship from becoming a back door for coordinated account creation.

Verification renews annually. A user whose verification lapses retains their account, content, and data, but loses the ability to post, comment, react, message, or vote until re-verified. Verification status is independent of data ownership; lapsed users may export their data at any time.

**Section 3.3 — Verification Levels**

The platform recognizes the following participation levels:

- **View-only:** Open to all visitors. No verification required. Read public content, browse profiles, observe communities. Available to ages thirteen (13) and older where legally permissible.
- **Verified Participant:** Government ID-verified, verification fee paid, age eighteen (18) or older. Full participation rights: post, comment, react, message, vote, follow, join communities.
- **Lapsed Verified Participant:** Previously verified but verification has expired. Read-only access to their own account; can re-verify at any time to restore full participation.
- **Trusted Member:** Verified Participant with sustained positive Ethos standing in one or more communities. Eligible for community moderation roles. Specific thresholds defined in the Ethos Methodology Document.
- **Governance-Eligible Member:** Trusted Member with minimum tenure as defined in the Ethos Methodology Document. Eligible for Board candidacy.

**Section 3.4 — Privacy Protection**

FLIPNET shall never sell, share, or expose user identity data to third parties except as required by applicable law pursuant to Article XIV. Identity verification shall be performed using privacy-preserving methods such that FLIPNET retains the minimum information necessary to confirm verification status — typically a verification token and expiration date — and not the underlying identity documents themselves. Verification data shall never be used for advertising purposes, content ranking, or any purpose beyond confirming verification status.

**Section 3.5 — One Account Policy**

Each verified individual is entitled to one (1) FLIPNET account. Creation of duplicate accounts is a violation of this Constitution and subject to permanent removal of all duplicate accounts. Account transfers between individuals are prohibited.

**Section 3.6 — Age Requirements**

FLIPNET participation is restricted to individuals aged eighteen (18) and older, verified through government-issued identification.

Individuals aged thirteen (13) and older may use FLIPNET in view-only capacity, where legally permissible under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations. View-only access requires no verification and no account creation. View-only users do not generate identity data subject to the protections in this Article.

The Board may establish additional age-related safeguards as needed to comply with evolving regulations.

## ARTICLE IV: ETHOS SYSTEM

**Section 4.1 — Purpose**

The Ethos system is FLIPNET's mechanism for reputation-weighted community moderation. It replaces opaque algorithmic moderation with a transparent, merit-based system where influence in moderation is earned through demonstrated accuracy and good faith — not through popularity, wealth, or tenure alone.

**Section 4.2 — Per-Community Ethos**

Ethos is measured independently within each community in which a user participates. A user may have a high Ethos score in a technology community and a neutral Ethos score in a political community. This reflects genuine expertise and participation within each domain and prevents power consolidation across communities.

**Section 4.3 — Ethos Mechanics**

Ethos rises and falls based on the accuracy and good faith of a user's actions within each community:

- Flagging content that is upheld upon review increases Ethos
- Flagging content that is dismissed upon review decreases Ethos
- Coordinated or mass flagging detected algorithmically results in Ethos penalty and temporary suspension of flagging rights
- Successful appeal of a user's own content removal decreases the Ethos of the flagging party
- Sustained accurate moderation over time increases Ethos toward the maximum
- Extended inactivity in moderation gradually decays Ethos toward neutral baseline

The specific scale, starting values, numerical weights, decay rates, and adjustment formulas shall be published in a public Ethos Methodology Document maintained by the Board and subject to community review. The methodology document is the operational implementation of the principles in this Article.

**Section 4.4 — Ethos and Platform Privileges**

Ethos determines moderation weight and eligibility for community and governance roles. Specific thresholds for moderation panel eligibility, governance candidacy, and other Ethos-gated privileges are defined in the Ethos Methodology Document.

No individual user's flag, regardless of Ethos score, shall result in automatic content removal or content holds without review by at least one additional moderator or automated review process. This safeguard prevents any single user from exercising unilateral censorship power.

**Section 4.5 — New Account Protections**

Newly verified accounts shall not carry meaningful flagging weight until they have demonstrated sustained good-faith participation within a community. Specific thresholds for new-account flagging weight are defined in the Ethos Methodology Document. This provision exists to prevent coordinated brigading by newly created accounts, even when those accounts are individually verified.

**Section 4.6 — Transparency**

A user's Ethos score in each community in which they participate is visible on their public profile. The criteria for Ethos changes, the methodology document, and the algorithmic systems that compute Ethos are published and publicly auditable. No hidden moderation scoring exists on FLIPNET.

## ARTICLE V: COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE

**Section 5.1 — Community Types**

FLIPNET supports two categories of community:

- **Public Communities:** Open to all verified users. Content is publicly visible.
- **Private Communities:** Membership by invitation or application. Content visible only to members. Private communities retain full governance rights.

**Section 5.2 — Community Rules**

Every FLIPNET community shall adopt and publish a set of Community Rules upon founding. Rules define the community's purpose, expected conduct, and moderation standards. Community Rules are short, plain-language, and publicly displayed. They may not violate this Constitution. Communities are encouraged to keep rules minimal in service of user freedom; the Constitution and platform-wide policies provide the baseline expectations.

**Section 5.3 — Community Moderators**

Each community shall elect its own moderators from among eligible members. Moderator elections shall be held annually. Community members may recall a moderator by petition requiring twenty-five percent (25%) of active community members, with removal by simple majority vote. FLIPNET platform staff may only intervene in community moderation in cases of clear Constitutional violation or to comply with applicable law.

**Section 5.4 — Community Revenue Participation**

Communities that contribute measurably to platform activity and value may receive a share of surplus revenue through the Community Distribution Fund, as determined by the Board's annual allocation plan and subject to community ratification. The methodology for calculating community contribution shall be published and transparent.

## ARTICLE VI: USER RIGHTS AND ACCOUNTABILITY

**Section 6.1 — User Rights**

Every verified FLIPNET user holds the following rights, which cannot be suspended except through the defined accountability process:

- The right to speak freely within community guidelines and this Constitution
- The right to know the reason for any moderation action taken against their content or account
- The right to appeal any moderation decision through the defined appeals process
- The right to their own data, including the right to export and delete it, subject to applicable data retention requirements
- The right to vote in community and platform elections, subject to the limits in Article VII
- The right to nominate and vote on surplus distributions
- The right to a transparent, documented accountability process before any permanent action

**Section 6.2 — Accountability Ladder**

FLIPNET recognizes that accountability must be proportional and rehabilitative wherever possible. The following graduated response system applies to violations:

- **Warning:** Documented notice of violation. No platform restrictions.
- **Content Restriction:** Specific content removed or restricted. Account otherwise intact.
- **Community Suspension:** Temporary removal from specific community. Appeal eligible.
- **Feature Restriction:** Temporary loss of specific platform privileges (posting, messaging, flagging) proportional to the violation. Account remains active.
- **Platform Suspension:** Temporary full-platform suspension. Appeal required for reinstatement.
- **Permanent Removal:** Reserved for the most severe violations. Account cannot be recreated.

**Section 6.3 — Accountability Records**

FLIPNET maintains records of moderation actions, outcomes, and appeal decisions as part of its platform integrity system. These records are visible to platform governance personnel and are used in evaluating patterns of behavior. Moderation records are retained for the duration of account activity and for a reasonable period thereafter as required for platform integrity and legal compliance. Personal data unrelated to moderation actions is subject to applicable data protection law, including the right to erasure where legally mandated. Good behavior over time improves standing, but moderation history informs future accountability decisions.

**Section 6.4 — Appeals Process**

Any user subject to a moderation action has the right to appeal. Appeals are reviewed by a randomly selected citizen panel drawn from users with sufficient Ethos standing in the relevant community, as defined in the Ethos Methodology Document. Panel decisions are final at the community level. Platform-level appeals may be escalated to the Board of Directors (or, prior to Foundation formation, to the Founding Chair) for cases involving alleged Constitutional violations.

## ARTICLE VII: FINANCIAL STRUCTURE AND COMMUNITY DISTRIBUTION

**Section 7.1 — Non-Extractive Finance Principle**

FLIPNET is built on the principle that the value created by a community should be returned to that community. No individual, investor, shareholder, or insider shall extract profit from FLIPNET. Surplus revenue — defined as all revenue remaining after operating expenses, staff compensation, debt service, mandated reserves, and approved capital expenditures — shall be distributed according to this Article. This principle is irrevocable.

For the avoidance of doubt: FLIPNET may and is expected to generate revenue, operate at a surplus, compensate its staff and contractors fairly, invest in platform development, and maintain financial reserves. What is prohibited is the extraction of that surplus by insiders for personal enrichment. The end goal of FLIPNET's financial model is to sustain the platform, pay for the service and its upkeep, and distribute remaining surplus back to the community.

**Section 7.2 — Revenue Sources**

FLIPNET may generate revenue through any combination of the following:

- **Verification Fees:** Annual fees paid by verified users covering identity verification costs and platform operating margin. Verification fees are the platform's primary revenue source.
- **Voluntary Subscriptions:** Optional patronage tiers for users who wish to contribute beyond the verification fee. Voluntary subscriptions do not unlock different platform features, content, or experiences. The platform is the same for everyone, regardless of payment level. Patronage, not pay-to-win.
- **Contextual Advertising:** Advertisements matched to the topic of content being viewed. No behavioral targeting. No identity-based targeting. No tracking pixels. Ever. Implemented only if and when traffic justifies it, subject to Board approval.
- **Donations and Grants:** Accepted from individuals, organizations, and institutions. Donations that impose conditions on FLIPNET's mission, governance, or editorial independence shall be declined.
- **Platform Services:** API access, business tools, verified business profiles, or other value-added services, provided they do not compromise user privacy or platform integrity and do not create a different platform experience for users versus non-users of those services.

The Board may authorize additional revenue sources not listed here, provided they do not conflict with the Irrevocable Prohibitions in §1.5 or the Non-Extractive Finance Principle in §7.1. All new revenue sources must be disclosed to the community.

The platform shall never sell, share, license, or transfer individual user data to any third party for any purpose. This is irrevocable.

**Section 7.3 — Financial Transparency**

FLIPNET shall publish a full, audited financial statement quarterly, visible to all verified users. The statement shall itemize operating expenses, revenue by source, staff compensation (in aggregate), reserve balances, capital expenditures, and distribution amounts. Annual audits shall be conducted by an independent third-party auditor.

**Section 7.4 — Operating Reserve**

FLIPNET shall maintain an operating reserve equal to no less than six (6) months of projected operating expenses. The Board may increase this minimum but may not decrease it below six months. Reserve funds may only be used for platform operations and approved capital expenditures.

During the period prior to the platform achieving a sustained operating surplus, the six-month reserve requirement is a target rather than a prerequisite for operation. The Founding Chair may self-fund operational shortfalls during this period, with such funding documented as either a contribution to the entity (non-recoverable) or a documented loan repayable from future surplus, at the Founding Chair's election. The platform shall not operate under any obligation that compromises the Irrevocable Prohibitions in §1.5.

**Section 7.5 — Surplus Distribution**

When FLIPNET generates surplus revenue after meeting all operating obligations and reserve requirements, that surplus shall be directed to the Community Distribution Fund. The Board shall propose an annual allocation plan specifying how the surplus is distributed among the following categories:

- **Community-Nominated Charitable Causes:** As determined by community vote.
- **Community Infrastructure Grants:** Directed to communities for locally-decided uses, platform improvement, or user-benefiting programs.
- **Emergency Response Fund:** A reserve for urgent community needs that cannot wait for scheduled distributions.
- **Platform Reinvestment:** Capital allocated to platform development, infrastructure, security, and growth, subject to Board discretion.

The allocation percentages among these categories shall be proposed by the Board and ratified by community vote annually. The Board may adjust allocations mid-year in response to extraordinary circumstances, subject to community disclosure within thirty (30) days. No fixed allocation percentages are constitutionally mandated, allowing the Board and community to adapt distribution priorities as the platform matures.

**Section 7.6 — Community Distribution Vote**

Every verified user holds votes in the surplus distribution, earned through positive platform participation:

- **Base allocation:** 1 vote per verified user
- **Additional votes earned through:** accurate flagging, moderation service, content contribution, and sustained tenure
- **Maximum vote allocation:** 3 votes per user, regardless of activity level
- **The three-vote maximum is a Constitutional provision and cannot be amended**

Users nominate causes during a defined nomination window. Nominations require minimum Ethos standing as defined in the Ethos Methodology Document, to prevent abuse. Nominated causes undergo a verification process. Votes are cast during a defined public voting period. Results and fund allocations are published in full.

**Section 7.7 — Emergency Response Fund**

The Board may authorize Emergency Response Fund distributions for urgent community needs that cannot wait for scheduled distribution cycles. Qualifying situations include immediate humanitarian need, natural disaster, or acute community crisis. Individual distributions from this fund are limited to no more than twenty percent (20%) of the fund's balance per event. All distributions are publicly disclosed within seven (7) days.

## ARTICLE VIII: PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

**Section 8.1 — Decentralized Infrastructure**

FLIPNET shall pursue decentralized infrastructure where technically feasible and operationally practical, ensuring resilience against single points of control, capture, or failure. Platform infrastructure decisions must be approved by the Board (or, prior to Foundation formation, the Founding Chair) and are subject to community review.

**Section 8.2 — Algorithmic Transparency**

FLIPNET shall publish, in plain language accessible to non-technical users, a full description of every algorithm that affects content visibility, user ranking, or moderation weighting. No hidden algorithmic systems shall operate on the platform. Community feeds default to chronological order. Algorithmic recommendations and ranked feeds are opt-in only.

**Section 8.3 — No Engagement Exploitation**

FLIPNET shall not employ design patterns, algorithmic systems, or notification strategies whose documented primary purpose is to maximize time-on-platform at the expense of user wellbeing. This includes but is not limited to: infinite scroll without user-defined limits, outrage amplification in content ranking, and dark pattern notification manipulation.

**Section 8.4 — User Data Rights**

Users own their data. FLIPNET holds it in trust. Users may export their complete data at any time. Users may delete their account and all associated data, with the exception of moderation records retained for platform integrity purposes as defined in §6.3 and subject to applicable data protection law. Data is never sold. Advertising data is contextual only and contains no individual user identifiers.

## ARTICLE IX: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Section 9.1 — Platform IP Ownership**

All intellectual property developed for or by FLIPNET — including but not limited to source code, platform architecture, trademarks, trade names, logos, and proprietary systems — is owned by FLIPNET LLC during the LLC operating period and shall be transferred to the Foundation upon its formation. Platform IP is held in trust for the community. No individual, including the Founding Chair, may claim personal ownership of platform IP developed during their service to FLIPNET.

**Section 9.2 — User Content Ownership**

Users retain ownership of all content they create and post on FLIPNET. By posting, users grant FLIPNET a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display, distribute, and cache their content as necessary for platform operation. Users may revoke this license by deleting their content. FLIPNET shall never claim ownership of user-generated content.

**Section 9.3 — Open Source Commitment**

FLIPNET's platform codebase is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). The codebase is publicly available from inception. This commitment is irrevocable. The use of AGPL-3.0 specifically protects FLIPNET's mission-driven code from being incorporated into commercial proprietary platforms without contributing back to the public commons.

The Board may authorize additional open-source releases of platform tools, infrastructure components, or related software under licenses appropriate to those components, provided no release compromises platform security in a way that endangers users.

**Section 9.4 — Trademark Protection**

The FLIPNET name, logo, and associated marks are registered trademarks (USPTO Serial #99725384, Class 042). Trademarks are owned by FLIPNET LLC during the LLC operating period and shall be transferred to the Foundation upon its formation. Use of FLIPNET trademarks by third parties requires written authorization from the entity holding them at the time of use. The trademark holder shall actively protect the marks against unauthorized use or dilution.

## ARTICLE X: DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY

**Section 10.1 — Governing Privacy Standards**

FLIPNET shall comply with all applicable data protection laws in jurisdictions where it operates, including but not limited to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nDSG/FADP), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Where laws conflict, FLIPNET shall default to the most protective standard.

**Section 10.2 — Data Breach Notification**

In the event of a data breach affecting user personal data, FLIPNET shall:

- Notify affected users within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery, consistent with GDPR Article 33 requirements
- Notify relevant supervisory authorities as required by applicable law
- Publish a public incident report within thirty (30) days detailing the nature, scope, and remediation of the breach
- Provide affected users with guidance on protective measures

The Board shall maintain a Data Breach Response Plan, reviewed and updated annually.

**Section 10.3 — Data Processing Principles**

FLIPNET shall process personal data only for specified, legitimate purposes disclosed to users at the time of collection. Data minimization shall be a guiding principle: FLIPNET shall collect and retain only the data necessary for platform operation and the purposes described in this Constitution. Data retention schedules shall be published and adhered to.

**Section 10.4 — International Data Transfers**

Where user data is transferred across jurisdictions, FLIPNET shall employ appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law, including Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or equivalent mechanisms approved by relevant supervisory authorities.

## ARTICLE XI: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**Section 11.1 — Internal Resolution**

Disputes between users, between users and moderators, or between communities shall be resolved through FLIPNET's internal moderation and appeals process as defined in Article VI before escalation to external mechanisms.

**Section 11.2 — External Arbitration**

Disputes that cannot be resolved internally, or disputes between FLIPNET and external parties, shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules of a recognized arbitration body.

**Prior to Foundation formation:** The seat of arbitration shall be Portland, Oregon, United States. Arbitration shall be conducted under the rules of the American Arbitration Association or a comparable body. The language of arbitration shall be English.

**Following Foundation formation:** The seat of arbitration shall be Zurich, Switzerland. Arbitration shall be conducted under the rules of the Swiss Chambers' Arbitration Institution or a comparable international arbitration body. The language of arbitration shall be English.

**Section 11.3 — Governing Law**

**Prior to Foundation formation:** This Constitution shall be governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, United States, and applicable federal law of the United States. FLIPNET LLC operates under Oregon law.

**Following Foundation formation:** This Constitution and the governance of the Foundation shall be governed by Swiss law. Operational matters of operational entities shall be governed by the laws of their respective jurisdictions. In the event of a conflict between Swiss Foundation law and operational entity law, the provisions of this Constitution shall take precedence to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

## ARTICLE XII: SUCCESSION AND CONTINUITY

**Section 12.1 — Founding Chair Succession**

**Prior to Foundation formation:** The Founding Chair shall maintain a current succession plan within the FLIPNET LLC operating agreement, identifying a designated successor and outlining the transfer of LLC membership in the event of the Founding Chair's death, permanent incapacity, or voluntary resignation. The succession plan shall be reviewed and updated annually. The designated successor shall be bound by this Constitution and shall continue the path toward Foundation formation. Upon Foundation formation, the LLC operating agreement succession plan is superseded by the Foundation's governance structure.

**Following Foundation formation:** The Founding Chair shall designate a successor in a sealed document held by the Foundation's legal counsel. In the event the Founding Chair is permanently incapacitated, deceased, or voluntarily resigns, the designated successor shall assume the role of Acting Chair for the remainder of the Founding Period, or if after the Founding Period, until a special election can be held. If no designated successor is available, the Board shall elect an Acting Chair from among its members by two-thirds (2/3) supermajority.

**Section 12.2 — Board Continuity**

In the event that the Board falls below quorum and cannot fill vacancies through the normal appointment or election process, the remaining Board members shall have emergency authority to appoint temporary members to restore quorum. Temporary members serve until the next scheduled election.

**Section 12.3 — Platform Wind-Down**

In the event that FLIPNET must permanently cease operations, the governing entity (LLC during the LLC period, Foundation thereafter) shall:

- Provide all users a minimum of ninety (90) days notice
- Enable complete data export for all users
- Distribute any remaining funds through the Community Distribution Fund process
- Open-source the platform codebase where legally permissible (the codebase is already AGPL-3.0 per §9.3)
- Transfer the domain and platform name to a designated successor organization that shares FLIPNET's mission, or retire them permanently

Wind-down requires a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority of the Board (or, prior to Foundation formation, the explicit decision of the Founding Chair) and ratification by a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority of verified users.

## ARTICLE XIII: EMPLOYEE AND CONTRACTOR GOVERNANCE

**Section 13.1 — Employment Standards**

FLIPNET shall compensate all employees and contractors fairly and in compliance with applicable labor laws. Compensation structures shall be transparent to the Board and reported in aggregate in quarterly financial statements.

**Section 13.2 — Whistleblower Protection**

FLIPNET shall protect any employee, contractor, or community member who in good faith reports violations of this Constitution, applicable law, or ethical standards. Retaliation against whistleblowers is prohibited and constitutes grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination. The Board shall maintain a confidential reporting channel independent of platform management.

**Section 13.3 — Contractor Obligations**

All contractors and service providers shall be bound by written agreements requiring compliance with FLIPNET's privacy, data protection, and mission requirements. Contractor agreements shall include appropriate confidentiality, intellectual property assignment, and termination provisions.

## ARTICLE XIV: LEGAL COMPLIANCE

**Section 14.1 — Law Enforcement and Government Requests**

FLIPNET shall comply with lawful orders from courts and regulatory authorities in jurisdictions where it operates. All government requests for user data or content removal shall be:

- Evaluated by legal counsel for validity and scope
- Complied with only to the minimum extent required by law
- Documented in a public transparency report, published at least annually, detailing the number, type, and jurisdiction of requests received and the platform's response
- Disclosed to affected users to the extent permitted by law

FLIPNET shall resist overbroad or legally insufficient requests through available legal channels. Government requests shall not be used as a pretext for content suppression that would otherwise violate this Constitution.

**Section 14.2 — DMCA and Copyright Compliance**

FLIPNET shall maintain a designated agent for receiving copyright takedown notices under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent international copyright frameworks. The platform shall implement a notice-and-takedown process that protects both copyright holders and users' rights to fair use, including a counter-notification process.

**Section 14.3 — Anti-Money Laundering**

To the extent FLIPNET handles financial transactions, including charitable distributions, the platform shall comply with applicable anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) regulations. The Board shall establish appropriate financial controls and reporting procedures.

**Section 14.4 — Tax Compliance**

FLIPNET LLC and, upon formation, the Foundation, along with all operational entities, shall comply with applicable tax laws in their respective jurisdictions. Financial structures shall be reviewed annually by qualified tax counsel to maintain compliance across all operating jurisdictions.

**Section 14.5 — Indemnification and Liability**

FLIPNET shall maintain appropriate insurance and indemnification provisions for Board members, officers, employees, and the Founding Chair acting in good faith within the scope of their duties. The Terms of Service shall include appropriate liability limitations and user indemnification provisions consistent with applicable law. The Board shall maintain a legal defense reserve as part of the operating budget.

## ARTICLE XV: CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS

**Section 15.1 — Amendment Process**

This Constitution may be amended by a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority vote of the Board of Directors, followed by ratification by a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority of verified users in a community-wide vote. Amendment votes shall be open for no less than thirty (30) days. Proposed amendments must be published for community review no less than sixty (60) days before a vote.

Prior to Foundation formation, amendments to this Constitution may be made by the Founding Chair, provided that no such amendment may weaken or remove any Irrevocable Provision listed in §15.2. All pre-Foundation amendments shall be documented in the public audit log and shall remain subject to community ratification once the Foundation is formed.

**Section 15.2 — Irrevocable Provisions**

The following provisions of this Constitution are irrevocable and cannot be amended under any circumstances:

- §1.5: Irrevocable Prohibitions
- §7.1: Non-Extractive Finance Principle
- §7.6: The three-vote maximum in community distribution
- §8.3: No Engagement Exploitation
- §3.5: One Account Policy
- §9.3: AGPL-3.0 Open Source Commitment
- The prohibition on premium features creating differential platform experiences (§1.5)
- The prohibition on selling, sharing, or licensing individual user data (§7.2)

Any proposed amendment to these sections shall be automatically void. The Board has no authority to submit such amendments for vote. The Founding Chair has no authority to enact such amendments unilaterally.

## ARTICLE XVI: FOUNDING COMMITMENT

FLIPNET was conceived in 2020 and built on the belief that the internet can be better — that human beings, when given honest tools and honest governance, are capable of building something worthy of the communication technology we have been given.

The Founding Chair commits to the following:

- To build FLIPNET in the open, with transparency at every stage
- To transition governance to a Swiss Foundation when the platform is ready and the right people exist to lead it
- To treat every user as a stakeholder, not a product
- To hold this Constitution as the highest authority, above personal interest
- To leave FLIPNET more capable, more independent, and more mission-aligned than it was found

The world does not need another platform that extracts value from human connection. It needs infrastructure worthy of human potential. FLIPNET is that attempt.

***Freedom. Liberty. Independence. Prosperity.***
